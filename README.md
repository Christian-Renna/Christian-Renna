### 🔬 Research
I work with Dr. Andreas Aristotelous (University of Akron) on computational biology, implementing a spatial model of bacterial infections treated with bacteriophages and antibiotics, with the goal of identifying effective combination-treatment strategies as antibiotic and phage resistance emerge.

The model simulates, on a 2D lattice:
- Bacterial growth, replication, local competition, and inherited resistance
- Individual phage particles moving via Brownian motion, adsorption, infection, latency, lysis, replication
- Antibiotic diffusion and concentration across the spatial domain
- Coupled PDE/ODE systems solved with finite difference methods (Euler, Crank–Nicolson, Runge–Kutta, ADI, BDF)

Built in Python with NumPy, SciPy, and Matplotlib.

### 🎲 DC Deck-Building Engine
A C++17 rules engine for a DC-Comics deck-building game. The engine is complete and audited with, cards, characters, effects, turn structure, scoring, destruction, attacking, and defending, with legal-action enumeration, privacy-safe observations, deterministic self-play, and a statistically grounded evaluation framework.
